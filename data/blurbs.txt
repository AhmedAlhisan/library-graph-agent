The Desert Star 1 introduces Noor, a young astronomer who discovers a strange light above the Empty Quarter. It is the first book in the series.

The Desert Star 2 continues the story that began in The Desert Star 1, following Noor as she crosses the desert to find the source of the light.

The Desert Star 3 is the final part of the trilogy and picks up right after Desert Star 2.

Riyadh Nights, written by Khalid Al-Otaibi and Huda Al-Fahad, is a standalone mystery set in old Riyadh. Readers who enjoyed The Desert Star 1 will love its atmosphere.

Code of the Dunes is a science fiction novel by Khalid Al-Otaibi. Dunes Reborn, planned for 2027, is the sequel to Code of the Dunes.
